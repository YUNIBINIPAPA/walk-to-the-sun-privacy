Privacy Policy Walk to the Sun · donalia Last updated: 2026-10-06 This policy covers the current version without ads, accounts or online sharing. Distances represent game progress. The app does not measure real steps or GPS location. Information stored on your device The app stores game distance, attendance, run usage and end times, memo text and drafts, creation and editing times, language selection and introduction viewing status. These support progress restoration and preferences. Memos are private by default and cannot be uploaded or shared. Transfers and protection The current app does not send saved data to developer servers or share it with third parties. It does not use advertising, payments, analytics or cloud crash reporting. Data is kept in local app storage without additional encryption. Please use device security features such as a screen lock. Retention and deletion Data remains while app storage is retained. To delete progress, memos and preferences, use Android Settings → Apps → Walk to the Sun → Storage → Clear data. The app has no recovery feature. Device or operating-system backup settings may restore data. The developer does not maintain a separate backup.
